Chapter 01 · What I should have said

Sami, I'm sorry.

I've thought about whether I should say all of this, and I realized that some things shouldn't stay unsaid.

I'm sorry for the ways I hurt you.

I'm sorry for the moments when my actions or words made you feel unimportant, misunderstood, or disappointed.

I could give explanations, but I don't want explanations to become excuses.

If I hurt someone I genuinely cared about, I have to be honest enough to admit that I was wrong.

So this is me saying it simply:
I'm sorry, Sami.
— Varun

Chapter 02 · More like a brother

Some friendships quietly become family.

You were never just someone I happened to know.

Over time, you became someone whose presence mattered to me.

Someone I could laugh with. Someone whose conversations became memories. Someone I thought of as a brother, even if we never shared the same blood.

Chapter 03 · No excuses

I'm not asking you to forget.

I'm not writing this to convince you that nothing happened.

I'm not asking you to ignore the things that hurt.

And I'm not trying to make myself look innocent.

I know that saying sorry doesn't automatically undo anything.

All I can do is acknowledge my part, learn from it, and be honest about how much I regret hurting someone I considered a brother.

Chapter 04 · If you need time

If someday you can forgive me…

I won't expect everything to instantly become the way it was.

I won't expect you to forget.

And I won't expect you to forgive me simply because I apologized.

If you need time, I understand.

If you need distance, I'll respect it.

And if someday you feel like talking again, I'll appreciate that opportunity.

This isn't a demand for another chance. It's simply an apology from someone who genuinely wishes he had handled things differently.

Whatever you choose, I respect it.
05 / 05

Chapter 05 · Final words

One last thing.

Whatever happens from here, I don't want the difficult moments to erase the good ones.

You were important to me.

The friendship mattered.

The memories mattered.

And I'm grateful for the time we had as friends.

Maybe people don't always realize the value of something until things become complicated. I wish I had appreciated it more while everything was simple.

I'm sorry, Sami.
Truly.
— Varun
